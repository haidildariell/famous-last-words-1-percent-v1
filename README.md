# Famous Last Words at 1%

A phone's final words at 1% battery, spoken in its own voice with a reputation to protect: instead of a farewell to the charger or a pun on the last percent, it insists — if anyone asks — that it died at full charge, turning a dying battery into a small, sharp piece of bravado.

> "If anyone asks, I died at 100%. I have a reputation to protect."

Run `python3 main.py` to write the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
